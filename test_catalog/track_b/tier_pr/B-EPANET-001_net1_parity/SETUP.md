# Water distribution network: what to build

EPANET Example Network 1: 9 junctions, a reservoir, a tank, 12 pipes and a pump over 24 hours. Your engine is compared with EPANET 2.2, element by element.

## Build
Nothing to build: run `inputs/network.inp` (EPANET format) as it is, with your engine's own defaults.

## Run
24 hours, reporting every hour. Report pressures in pascals and flows in m³/s.
