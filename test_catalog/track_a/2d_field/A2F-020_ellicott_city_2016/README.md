# A2F-020 — Ellicott City, Maryland: flash floods of 2016 and 2018

USGS [Fact Sheet 2021-3025](https://doi.org/10.3133/fs20213025) documents indirect peak-discharge measurements and high-water marks on the three streams through the historic district.

The 2016 flood is for calibration; the 2018 flood is scored with that set-up unchanged.

**Status: draft.** The next steps are to obtain the high-water mark and indirect-measurement data from USGS, and to assemble terrain and rainfall.
