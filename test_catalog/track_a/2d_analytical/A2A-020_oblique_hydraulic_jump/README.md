# A2A-020 — Oblique hydraulic jump

## Physics

Supercritical shallow-water flow deflected by a wall produces an oblique
hydraulic jump. Shock angle and conjugate depth follow Ippen (1951).

## Citation

Ippen, A. T. (1951). "Mechanics of supercritical flow." *Trans. ASCE*, 116,
268–295. Also catalogued in SWASHES (Delestre et al. 2013).
