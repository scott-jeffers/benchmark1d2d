# Water hammer: valve slammed shut: what to build

Water flows at 1 m/s through a 1 km pipe from a reservoir to a valve. The valve shuts instantly and a pressure wave of exactly 1 MPa (about 102 m of water) runs back up the pipe.

## Build
- One straight, horizontal pipe 1,000 m long, frictionless.
- Pressure wave speed 1,000 m/s. Water density 1,000 kg/m³.
- Reservoir at x = 0 holding the pressure constant; valve at x = 1,000 m.

## Start
- Velocity 1 m/s everywhere, pressure 0 (report pressures relative to this starting pressure).
- The valve closes completely at t = 0.

## Run
0.5 s of simulated time (the wave is half-way up the pipe), on four grids: computational reaches of 20, 10, 5 and 2.5 m.
