# A12-012 — Surcharge onto the surface and back

## Physics

A storm hydrograph rising to 0.3 m³/s at 10 minutes and back to zero at
20 minutes (180 m³) enters a 0.5 m pipe to manhole M1, whose outgoing 0.25 m
pipe carries only about 0.06 m³/s. M1 surcharges onto a walled 20 × 20 m
hollow whose floor falls 1 % towards the manhole from every side; once the
storm passes, the water returns down the manhole and out.

* **Discharged volume**: everything that entered, 180 m³, leaves by the
  outfall within three hours.
* **Surface empty**: the manhole is at the lowest point of the hollow, so no
  water can stay on the surface.

## What it checks

Exchange in both directions at one manhole, and that the round trip loses or
gains no water.

## Citation

Chen, A. S., Djordjević, S., Leandro, J., and Savić, D. A. (2007). The urban
inundated area: a coupled 1D/2D model for the interaction between sewer and
surface. *Proc. NOVATECH 2007*.
