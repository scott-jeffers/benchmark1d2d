# A1F-030 — Weber 90° junction

## Physics

Combining open-channel flow at a 90° junction. Separation zones and
contraction affect depths and velocities in the junction region.

## Citation

Weber, L. J., Schumate, E. D., and Mawer, N. (2001). "Experiments on flow at
a 90° open-channel junction." *J. Hydraul. Eng.*, 127(5), 340–350.
https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9429(2001)127:5(340)
