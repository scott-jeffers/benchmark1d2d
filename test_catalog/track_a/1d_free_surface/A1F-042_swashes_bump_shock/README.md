# A1F-042 — Transcritical flow over a bump with a hydraulic jump (SWASHES)

## Physics

The same channel and bump with \(q = 0.18\) m²/s and 0.33 m held
downstream. The flow goes critical at the crest, supercritical down its far
side and jumps back to subcritical where the Rankine–Hugoniot condition

\[ \frac{q^2}{h_1} + \frac{g h_1^2}{2} = \frac{q^2}{h_2} + \frac{g h_2^2}{2} \]

holds between the supercritical branch of the crest head and the subcritical
branch of the downstream head: \(x_s = 11.6656\) m (solved to \(10^{-12}\) m).

## What it checks

Shock capturing on a sloping bed: the jump's position and the conjugate
depths on either side.

## Citation

Delestre et al. (2013), SWASHES, doi:10.1002/fld.3741.
