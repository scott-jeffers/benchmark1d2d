# Dam break onto dry ground: what to build

A dam holding back 1 m of water vanishes at once and the water rushes onto a dry, flat, frictionless bed.

## Build
- A 1D channel 2,000 m long (x from 0 to 2,000 m), unit width, flat bed at z = 0.
- No friction.

## Start
- Water 1 m deep for x < 1,000 m, dry for x > 1,000 m, everything at rest.

## Boundaries
- Both ends open (let waves leave). The water never reaches them in this run.

## Run
60 s of simulated time, on four grids: cells of 10, 5, 2.5 and 1.25 m.
