# A1F-041 — Transcritical flow over a bump, no shock (SWASHES)

## Physics

The same channel and bump with \(q = 1.53\) m²/s. The flow is critical at
the crest (\(h_c = (q^2/g)^{1/3}\), head \(1.5 h_c + 0.2\)), subcritical
upstream and supercritical downstream, with no jump. As in SWASHES, 0.66 m
is held downstream only while the outflow is subcritical: once the flow
leaving the channel is supercritical it needs no downstream condition. Each
depth is the subcritical (upstream of the crest) or supercritical root of
the Bernoulli cubic.

## What it checks

The switch through critical flow at a control section, where many schemes
produce a glitch or settle on the wrong branch.

## Citation

Delestre et al. (2013), SWASHES, doi:10.1002/fld.3741.
