# Dam break into shallow water: what to build

The same dam break, but the water downstream of the dam is already 0.1 m deep, so a bore (a moving step) forms.

## Build
- A 1D channel 2,000 m long (x from −1,000 to +1,000 m), unit width, flat bed at z = 0.
- No friction.

## Start
- Water 1 m deep for x < 0 and 0.1 m deep for x > 0, everything at rest.

## Boundaries
- Both ends open. The waves do not reach them in this run.

## Run
30 s of simulated time, on four grids: cells of 10, 5, 2.5 and 1.25 m.
